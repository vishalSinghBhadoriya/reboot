# Assets

- `public/images/hero.webp` and its responsive version are original AI-generated concept artwork created for REBOOT. The exploded phone is a generic illustration.
- `screen-removed.webp` and `screen-installed.webp` are aligned detail crops of [IPhonedriod.Repairservice’s 02.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IPhonedriod.Repairservice%E2%80%99s_02.jpg) by Phonerepair1. Both WebP crops are available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). They document a third-party display replacement.
- The interactive hero hardware, screen shader, and scanner rings are original Three.js geometry. The earlier exploded-view concept image serves as a still fallback.
- Catalogue WebP files are crops of original AI-generated studio contact sheets. The smartphone galleries use separate concept designs and detail crops. Accessory images illustrate their categories. Replace these with authorized SKU-specific photos for actual inventory.
- The REBOOT mark and favicon were made for this project in CSS and SVG.
- Space Grotesk, Sora and IBM Plex Mono are distributed by Fontsource under the SIL Open Font License. Refer to bundled font package licenses. Icons are from Lucide under its package license.
